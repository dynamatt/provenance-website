Nothing here reinvents e-signatures

Signer identity, timestamps, reauthentication, and tamper-evidence are established, expected capabilities, and Provenance's signature design draws directly on how the mature implementations already do it. The real gaps are structural: an open, user-accessible storage format; a hosting model that doesn't default to a vendor's cloud; enforcement that actually blocks a bad change instead of just flagging it; and an offline artifact a reviewer can open without installing anything.

---
title: "Core concepts"
description: "The records and relationships at the center of Provenance."
---

Provenance represents design-control information as versioned records in a
customer-owned Git repository. Schemas describe record types, and typed links
connect records into a traceability graph.

Read about [records and links]({{< relref "docs/concepts/records-and-links" >}}),
[writing a schema]({{< relref "docs/concepts/schema" >}}) and the role of
[Git in the audit trail]({{< relref "docs/concepts/git-and-audit-trail" >}}).
